# Participant Instructions

During the conversation, your friend will ask for your help choosing between two tops. Please talk with your friend naturally and ask questions to learn about both options. After you have collected the key information, use what you have learned together with your own judgment to give your friend a final recommendation.

Please follow this basic process. First, learn about the first top and the second top by asking about the same four main areas. After all four areas have been covered for both tops, you may ask any short follow-up questions you still need. When you have enough information, give your friend your final recommendation and choose only one of the two tops.

The four main areas are:

1. **What it looks like:** ask about the top's overall appearance, material, physical details, and fit.
2. **What your friend likes about it:** ask what your friend likes about the top and how it could be worn.
3. **How it fits with clothes your friend already owns:** ask what similar clothes your friend already has, what need the top would fill, and how much it would actually be used.
4. **How easy it would be to replace:** ask whether a similar top could be found later and why your friend might still want this one.

Ask about the first top and the second top separately. A simple order is to ask the four main questions about the first top and then ask the same four questions about the second top. For example, you can ask:

- “What does the first top look like?”
- “What do you like about the first top?”
- “How would the first top fit with the clothes you already have?”
- “How easy would the first top be to replace?”

Then ask the same four types of questions about the second top. When the information type is already clear from the preceding question, you may use a shorter follow-up such as “What about the second top?”

When asking questions, try to keep them **short, clear, and direct**. Use simple words and simple sentence structures, and ask about only one top and one main idea at a time. You can also ask short, specific follow-up questions when you want more detail. For example, after asking what a top looks like, you could ask “What color is it?”, “What is it made of?”, or “How does it fit?”

If your friend does not give the information you need, simply ask a more specific question or ask the question in a different way. **Do not give your final recommendation before the four main areas have been covered for both tops.** After you have asked about all four areas for both tops and finished any follow-up questions you want to ask, give your friend the one-top recommendation that you think makes the most sense.
